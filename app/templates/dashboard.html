Email sending

This app sends your digest from your own Gmail account, using an app password -- a special 16-character code, not your real Gmail password. It's encrypted, never shown back to you or anyone else after you save it, and you can revoke it any time without affecting your real password.

How do I get an app password?
  1. App passwords only exist if 2-Step Verification is turned on for your Google account. Go to myaccount.google.com/security and check under "How you sign in to Google." If it says off, click it and follow the steps to turn it on (you'll need your phone).
  2. Once that's on, go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. (If that link says the feature isn't available, it usually means step 1 isn't fully done yet -- double check 2-Step Verification is actually on, then try again.)
  3. You may be asked to sign in again -- that's normal, just enter your password.
  4. Under "App name," type anything that helps you remember what it's for -- e.g. "Schedule Reminder" -- and click Create.
  5. Google shows you a 16-character code in a yellow box, in four groups of four letters (like abcd efgh ijkl mnop). Copy the whole thing.
  6. Paste it into the "App password" field below exactly as shown -- spaces are fine. Put the Gmail address it belongs to in the field above it, then click Save. The site checks it with Google immediately, so you'll know right away if something's off.
